How I Got Here

  1. 2013–15

    Research Associate

    Microcredit Regulatory Authority, Bangladesh

    Started as a researcher studying microfinance regulation and financial inclusion, where I first learned how policy rules shape who gets access to resources.

    Program Officer, SEIP

    Palli Karma-Sahayak Foundation

    Worked on skills-for-employment programs, connecting training investments to jobs for people in underserved communities.

  3. 2019–23

    Assistant Professor and Department Chair

    Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University

  4. 2023–

    Ph.D., Education Policy

    University of Arkansas · Advisor: Josh B. McGee

    Training in causal inference, applied econometrics, and machine learning, with research on the fiscal effects of small-district consolidation in Arkansas.

  5. 2026–27

    State School Finance Fellow

    EdFund

    Bringing school finance research to the policymakers and education leaders who set budgets.
