Job Market Paper

"When Small Districts Disappear: Fiscal Consequences of Consolidation in Arkansas"

with Josh B. McGee · EDRE Working Paper, University of Arkansas [No. TBD]

This paper studies the fiscal consequences of school district consolidation using administrative data on Arkansas public school districts. Exploiting variation in the timing and scale of district consolidations, I estimate how merging districts affects per-pupil spending, the allocation of spending between administration and instruction, and measures of operating efficiency.
