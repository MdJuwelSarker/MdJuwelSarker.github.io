Service

University & Departmental Service

2025–2026 · Department of Education Reform, University of Arkansas

Department Representative, Graduate-Professional Student Congress (GPSC)

I represent the graduate students of the Department of Education Reform within the university's Graduate-Professional Student Congress, serving as a liaison between my department and the broader graduate student body. In this role, I relay departmental concerns and priorities to GPSC, participate in decisions affecting graduate student funding, policy, and campus life, and keep my department informed of university-wide initiatives and opportunities relevant to graduate students.

2021–2023 · Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University (HSTU)

Founder, DevTalk

As a course teacher in the Department of Development Studies, HSTU, I kept encountering the same gap: students graduating with strong theoretical grounding but little direct contact with the sector they hoped to enter. DevTalk was my response — an academia–industry collaboration initiative connecting students with practitioners and employers in Bangladesh's development sector, including Eco-Social Development Organization, Friendship International, and Save the Children. From 2021 to 2023, every batch participated in field visits to gain first-hand experience of development practice, more than 50 students gained research and data collection experience, and the initiative hosted three invited DevTalk workshops featuring practitioners and employers from these partner organizations — including “DevTalk-2: Career in Development Sector (Public Health)” (August 2022).

Covered by Channel 24.

2020 · Rajshahi & Rangpur Region, Bangladesh (via Zoom)

COVID-19 Webinar Series, Development Studies Research Group

When COVID-19 forced university closures across Bangladesh, I co-organized a series of 16 webinars, bringing together faculty and professionals from across the country to grapple with the pandemic's disruption in real time. Rather than treating resilience as an abstract concept, the series centered lived experience — including “Stories of Vulnerabilities and Resilience: Reflections by Development Studies Students” (May 2020), a session in which students became the voices of the conversation, sharing how they navigated uncertainty, loss, and adaptation during one of the most disorienting periods in recent memory.

Covered by Prothom Alo: April 2020, May 2020.

2020–2021 · Bangladesh (nationwide)

Founder, Bangladesh Open Learning Society (BOLS)

When COVID-19 shut down universities across Bangladesh, I founded the Bangladesh Open Learning Society to ensure the closures didn't mean a pause in learning. The community grew to 10,000+ members, who received free access to courses from Coursera and edX. Members went on to complete 70,000+ learning hours and earn 4,500+ certificates from 200+ universities — including Harvard, MIT, and Stanford — via Coursera, edX, and DataCamp. Hosted sessions included “Online Learning in Coursera Campus: Experience of Amazing Learners” (May 2020).

Covered by The Daily Naya Shatabdi and Ittefaq.

2020 · Department of Development Studies, HSTU

Undergraduate Curriculum Redesign & Leadership

I coordinated the design and development of the undergraduate curriculum in Development Studies, leading consultations across academic colleagues, industry partners, and university leadership to align academic standards with field relevance. The department adopted the curriculum in 2020. Upon becoming Department Chair, I directed its transition to an Outcome-Based Education (OBE) framework — authoring core syllabi and learning outcomes, and mentoring faculty through pedagogical alignment and implementation.
